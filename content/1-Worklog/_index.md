This worklog covers the 12-week internship in the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program, starting on 14 September 2026, in two phases.

**Phase 1 — Learning (weeks 1–8).** I worked through 28 daily AWS study topics grouped into eight weeks: account and cost safety, IAM and VPC, compute and storage, monitoring and databases, operations and infrastructure as code, security and messaging, containers, CI/CD, and serverless patterns.

**Phase 2 — Project (weeks 9–12).** I applied these foundations to **AskFirst**, a serverless Telegram assistant that helps older adults and their families check suspicious messages before transferring money. The project is described in the [Proposal](../2-proposal/) and built step by step in the [Workshop](../5-workshop/).

**Week 1 (14–18 September 2026):** [Onboarding, account and cost safety, IAM and VPC](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2 (21–25 September 2026):** [EC2, S3, RDS, Lightsail, and Auto Scaling](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3 (28 September–2 October 2026):** [CloudWatch, Route 53, CLI, DynamoDB, CloudFront, and Lambda](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4 (5–9 October 2026):** [Tags, Systems Manager, CloudFormation, CDK, and right-sizing](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5 (12–16 October 2026):** [Billing, quotas, EBS lifecycle, WAF, Secrets Manager, and Cognito](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6 (19–23 October 2026):** [Backup, VPC peering, SQS/SNS, Docker, and ECS](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7 (26–30 October 2026):** [CI/CD, cost analytics, Lambda + DynamoDB, API Gateway, and SAM](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8 (2–6 November 2026):** [CDN, search, tracing, ECS/Fargate, EKS, and consolidation](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9 (9–13 November 2026):** [AskFirst — requirements, architecture, and foundation](1.9-week9/)

**Week 10 (16–20 November 2026):** [AskFirst — Java webhook and worker Lambdas](1.10-week10/)

**Week 11 (23–27 November 2026):** [AskFirst — AI Lambda and end-to-end flow](1.11-week11/)

**Week 12 (30 November–4 December 2026):** [AskFirst — security, operations, testing, and wrap-up](1.12-week12/)
